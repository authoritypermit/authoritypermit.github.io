Florida shed and metal building permit checklist

Before ordering a shed or metal building, gather the property address, building dimensions, intended use, proposed location, and any drawings from the supplier. Ask the local permit office what it needs for that specific project.

Authority Permit LLC · November 20, 2026

What should I ask the supplier for?

Ask for the product dimensions, drawings or specifications available for that model, and any information on how the building will be installed or anchored. Tell the supplier if you plan to add electrical or plumbing work.

What should I show on the site sketch?

Mark the proposed location and any features you already know about, such as property lines, existing structures, easements, a well, or a septic system. Ask the reviewing office what it needs on the final site plan.

Can Authority Permit help an owner-builder?

Authority Permit helps with paperwork for eligible owner-builder projects. Share the address and what you plan to build so we can discuss the next step. The local office can confirm the requirements and whether owner-builder filing applies to your project.
