Where to start with permits in Williston and Levy County

Start with the property address and confirm whether the parcel is inside the City of Williston or in unincorporated Levy County. A Williston mailing address alone is not enough to choose a permit office.

Authority Permit LLC · November 20, 2026

Which office should I contact?

For a property inside city limits, start with the City of Williston building permit checklist. For unincorporated Levy County, start with Levy County Development Services. Confirm the right office and current forms directly before filing.

What should I have ready?

  • The street address or parcel number
  • A short description of the work and where it will sit on the property
  • Any drawings, site plan, equipment specifications, or existing permit number

How can Authority Permit help?

Authority Permit LLC helps homeowners and contractors gather paperwork, prepare applications, and coordinate filing and follow-up within the agreed scope. The agency determines which requirements apply and whether to issue a permit.
