Authority Permit is a Florida permit consulting firm. We prepare, submit, and track permit applications on behalf of contractors, utility providers, and homeowners.

Our concentration is in central Florida — Marion, Lake, Sumter, Citrus, and Levy counties — with filings across all 67 counties in the state.
