About Authority Permit

Meet Authority Permit.

Authority Permit LLC provides statewide permit management for contractors, homeowners, and businesses.

What we do

We handle the details that make permitting time-consuming: figuring out where to file, gathering the right paperwork, and keeping you informed as your application moves through review.

We coordinate with the appropriate licensed professionals where the project requires them. Permit decisions are made by the reviewing jurisdiction.

Where we work

We serve projects statewide, including Marion, Levy, Citrus, Lake, and Sumter counties.
