What to gather before you ask for permit help

Start with the project location, a short scope of work, and any plans or product information you already have.

Read the article

Tell us the city or county, describe what you’re building or installing, and share any dates that affect your planning. If the job involves a prefabricated building or standby generator, keep the manufacturer’s specifications handy.

If you already applied, include the permit number and the most recent reviewer comments. That gives Authority Permit a clearer picture of what is outstanding.
